About this calendar

Every operational deadline and rhythm at the Maastricht Science Programme, in one place: exams, grading, BTR, Board of Examiners, scheduling, ESD and alumni, plus UM holidays and the period structure. Maintained by MSP Operations. Events marked unconfirmed are pending confirmation for this academic year and stay out of the feeds until confirmed.

Subscribe in Outlook

Click a feed and pick how to subscribe: one click for UM Outlook (Microsoft 365), webcal for Apple or desktop clients, or copy the URL. Updates appear in your calendar automatically, no re-downloading.
